Connect your Google Sheet to go live

Right now the page uses sample data built in. To use your own Sheet:
1. Make your Google Sheet public (Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer)
2. Get the Sheet ID from the URL: docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/SHEET_ID/edit
3. Open index.html, find SHEET_ID near the top, and paste yours in
4. Save and re-deploy to Vercel — done ✓
